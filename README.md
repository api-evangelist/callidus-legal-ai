Callidus Legal AI, now operating as StrongSuit, provides an advanced AI-driven legal research and litigation platform. It offers case law search, contract drafting, document review, oral argument simulation, and workflow automation for lawyers and legal teams. The platform integrates a comprehensive U.S. case law database with generative AI to streamline research, drafting, and risk management, targeting appellate litigators and corporate legal departments.

**Areas:** Legal Tech · Artificial Intelligence · Litigation · ContractAutomation · Research

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Callidus Legal AI: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
